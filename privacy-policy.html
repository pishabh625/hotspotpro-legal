Privacy Policy

Effective: September 23, 2026 · gemxd · TV Hotspot Pro: WiFi Tether ("the App")

gemxd built the TV Hotspot Pro: WiFi Tether App as a commercial app. The App is provided by gemxd after purchase and is intended for use as is.

Location Data and Use

The App requires your device's location (or nearby-devices permission on newer Android versions) to create a local Wi-Fi Direct group network, which is the main purpose of the application. Location access is required only to create the Wi-Fi group network, as required by the operating system. Your location is not used outside of this function and is never collected, tracked, or shared in any way.

Foreground Service Usage

The App uses a foreground service to perform its primary function: running an immediate, long-running Wi-Fi Direct hotspot for connected devices so they can exchange networking data through the on-device proxy. The App does not use a foreground service for any other purpose.

Boot Auto-Start

The App offers an optional toggle to start the hotspot automatically after the TV restarts. This preference is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

Information Collection and Use

This App does not collect any data. Your hotspot name, password, settings, and usage stay private, on your device, only accessible by you. Network traffic you choose to route through the proxy passes through your own device and is never logged, stored, or shared with gemxd or any other party, except where you voluntarily send data to the developer for troubleshooting (for example crash reports or usability issues).
